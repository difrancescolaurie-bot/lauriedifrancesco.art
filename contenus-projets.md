## ABOUT — BIO (Lot X, 2026-08-09)

### FR

**Origines**
Laurie Di Francesco est photographe et autrice, largement autodidacte.
Elle a grandi entre la France et l'Italie, puis a découvert le Brésil, qui est devenu une troisième terre d'accueil.

**Approche**
Elle met en images des récits intimes qui traitent des déplacements, géographiques d'abord, intérieurs ensuite. Ses projets illustrent les mouvements qui précèdent une transformation et interrogent régulièrement la notion de seuil, comme l'invitation d'une ligne à dépasser.
Son langage est poétique, sensoriel et philosophique. Elle pense et s'exprime en plusieurs langues, dont elle travaille les rythmes.

**Travaux**
Elle travaille actuellement sur deux séries. *Quando sono qui, non sono là* interroge la possibilité d'une renaissance après une enfance empêchée. *La Mer en Corps* documente le rapport d'un corps affaibli à la mer, en mêlant effort physique, nouvelles technologies et photographie argentique.
Elle a exposé pour la première fois au Brésil, à Belo Horizonte, en 2024, et a rejoint en 2026 le collectif français Monolit.

**Rangée 3, texte de projet**
Eyebrow : The project

The title stayed in Italian. A sentence spoken in passing, about a double belonging become triple, since adopted as an echo of an identity made of many shores.

In this project, the geographical shore becomes an inner shore.

Everything ends in a cave. Chosen, this one, not endured: a protected natural site, its forms drawn by water and time, a place one enters as into an enveloping, fertile shelter. On its walls, rock paintings. Lines that look almost childlike, thousands of years old, protected now, and that no one would think of burning anymore.

There is a deep, faraway territory, indigenous lands protected and defended. That is where the faces come from: dignity, anger, peace regained. There one meets an innocence, under the light of the cerrado, of the tropics, where the end of the day carries another density.

For all of this, it was necessary to return: to find again the marks of a millennial past that nothing and no one has erased, to meet faces that express the cycles of a life, hindered first, freed after. One can always make something of what has been made of us.

The series follows the movement of the allegory: shadows taken for the real, the descent, the fear that settles in, then the way out. It does not seek to prove and asks for nothing: it passes through.

The question it raises is simple: how does one hold on, after a childhood in which the gaze of adults was a tribunal. These images do not answer by bearing witness. They are made from outside, by a gaze that no longer mistakes shadows for the real.

To transfigure the real: that is where these images are made from. We have art in order not to die of the truth.

## TOWARD THE BLUE

### EN

Surtitre : dreaming
Titre : Toward / the Blue

**Rangée 1, citation 1**
Auteur : Rainer Maria Rilke · Letters to a Young Poet
Note : freely translated from the Brazilian edition

To look at things the way a child looks at something strange, from the depth of one's own world.

**Rangée 1, citation 2**
Auteur : Friedrich Nietzsche · Thus Spoke Zarathustra
Note : freely translated from the French edition

The child is innocence and forgetting, a new beginning, a game, a wheel rolling out of itself, a first movement, a sacred yes.

**Rangée 2, texte de projet**
Eyebrow : The project

The series opens on the gaze of a young girl and closes on the face of an adult woman. In between: stone dragons, a slide seen too close, panes of glass breaking the light apart, tarpaulins, reflections. Almost no nightmare. But the veil is there.

The blue of the sky runs through this body of work as a conceptual operator. It is the same from one end of the earth to the other, above wars as above games. Perhaps that is why it holds: it promises nothing, and it does not give way.

This series belongs to the current of magical realism. A tropical gaze rather than a Brazilian one, the gaze of someone who lived in Latin America long enough for the marvellous to stop being an exception.

There is indeed a passage here, from child to adult. But it does not tell how one grows up. It rewrites that passage where childhood was missing, and seeks to recover, after the fact, a lightness that never took place.

In Nietzsche, the child does not come first. It comes after the camel's burden and after the lion's refusal.

Chez Nietzsche, l'enfant ne vient pas en premier. Il vient après le fardeau du chameau et après le refus du lion.

Il bambino è innocenza e oblio, un ricominciamento, un gioco, una ruota che gira da sé, un primo movimento, un sacro sì.

**Rangée 2, texte de projet**
Eyebrow : IL PROGETTO

La serie si apre sullo sguardo di una ragazza e si chiude sul volto di una donna adulta. Nel mezzo: draghi di pietra, uno scivolo visto troppo da vicino, vetri che scompongono la luce, teloni, riflessi. Quasi nessun incubo. Ma il velo c'è.

L'azzurro del cielo attraversa tutto questo lavoro come un operatore concettuale. È lo stesso da un capo all'altro della terra, sopra le guerre come sopra i giochi. Forse è per questo che tiene: non promette nulla e non cede.

Questa serie si inscrive nella corrente del realismo magico. Uno sguardo tropicale più che brasiliano, quello di chi ha vissuto in America Latina abbastanza a lungo perché il meraviglioso smetta di essere un'eccezione.
